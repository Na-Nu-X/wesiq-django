What Personal Information We Collect

When You create an Account, complete Your profile or use the features of the Service, We may collect:

  • Account information: email address, username, password (stored only in hashed form) and, optionally, Your first and last name and phone number.
  • Profile information: profile picture, bio, preferred language and the links You choose to add to Your profile.

  • Messages: chat messages and attachments You send to other users.
  • Training and activity information: training sessions, training plans, completed tasks, activity records and the experience points (XP), levels and streaks calculated from them.
  • Social connections: the users You follow, the users who follow You and friend codes.
  • Communications with Us: information You provide when You contact Us, report content or request support.

If You choose to sign up or log in with Google, We receive Your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier from Google, as permitted by Your Google account settings.

Information Collected While Using the Application

With Your prior permission, the Application may access pictures and videos from Your Device's camera and photo library so that You can upload them to the Service. You can enable or disable this access at any time in Your Device settings.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when You use the Service. It may include Your Device's IP address, browser type and version, the pages of the Service You visit, the time and date of Your visit, the time spent on those pages, Device type, operating system, unique Device identifiers and other diagnostic data.

Purchase Information

When You make a purchase, We receive confirmation of the transaction, the product or subscription purchased, its status and renewal date. We do not receive or store Your full payment card details. See Payments and In-App Purchases below.

Cookies, Analytics and Tracking

Cookies

The Website uses the following categories of Cookies:

  • Strictly necessary Cookies: session, security (CSRF protection), language preference and cookie-consent Cookies. They are required for the Website to work and do not require Your consent.
  • Analytics Cookies: set by Google Analytics only after You select "Accept all" in the cookie banner. You can change Your choice at any time through the privacy link in the Website footer.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to measure and analyze traffic, primarily on the Website associated with the Application. Google Analytics helps Us understand how visitors use the Website, for example which pages are visited and how long visitors stay, so that We can improve the Service. Google Analytics is loaded only with Your consent, and We do not use it for advertising.

For more information on how Google uses this data, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with the browser add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Spam and Abuse Protection

Some forms on the Website are protected by Google reCAPTCHA, which collects hardware and software information (such as Device and browser data) and sends it to Google for analysis. Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers send "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals. Because there is no common industry standard for responding to DNT signals, the Service does not currently respond to them. However, We do not track You across third-party websites over time, and We do not allow third parties to do so through the Service for advertising purposes. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals where required by applicable law.

Your Rights Under the GDPR (European Users)

If You are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or equivalent local law applies to Our processing of Your Personal Data.

Legal Bases for Processing

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): to create and operate Your Account, provide the features of the Service, send service emails and process purchases.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): for analytics Cookies (Google Analytics), access to Your camera and photo library, location attached to posts and marketing emails. You may withdraw Your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): to keep the Service secure, prevent spam, fraud and abuse, and improve the Service, provided these interests are not overridden by Your rights.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): to comply with tax, accounting and other legal requirements.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR, You have the right to:

  • Access Your Personal Data and receive a copy of it;
  • Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data;
  • Erasure of Your Personal Data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Restriction of processing;
  • Data portability, that is, to receive Your Personal Data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and to transmit it to another controller;
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to object at any time to processing for direct marketing;
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent; and
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU Member State of Your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement. In Slovakia, this is the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (dataprotection.gov.sk).

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects concerning You. We respond to requests within one month, which may be extended by two further months where necessary, in which case We will inform You. We may need to verify Your identity before responding.
