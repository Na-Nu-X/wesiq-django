What Personal Information We Collect

Information You Provide to Us

When You create an Account, complete Your profile or use the features of the Service, We may collect:

  • Account information: email address, username, password (stored only in hashed form) and, optionally, Your first and last name and phone number.
  • Profile information: profile picture, bio, preferred language and the links You choose to add to Your profile.
  • Social media profile information: if You choose to display or link Your social media profiles in Your Account bio links, We process the links, usernames or handles You add from platforms such as Facebook or X (formerly Twitter). We do not access Your social media accounts, and We do not collect any data from those platforms. We only store and display the links You enter yourself, and they are visible to other users according to Your profile settings.
  • User content: posts, photos, videos, comments, reviews, reactions, saved posts and other content You publish or upload, including an optional location You choose to attach to a post.
  • Messages: chat messages, reactions, attachments (photos, videos and files) and voice messages You send to other users, and posts or articles You share in a chat.
  • Training and activity information: training sessions, training plans, completed tasks, activity records and the experience points (XP), levels and streaks calculated from them.
  • Social connections: the users You follow, the users who follow You and friend codes.
  • Communications with Us: information You provide when You contact Us, report content or request support.

Information from Third-Party Sign-In

If You choose to sign up or log in with Google on the Website, We receive Your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier from Google, as permitted by Your Google account settings.

Information Collected While Using the Application

The Application asks for the following permissions only when You use the related feature. You can grant or revoke each of them at any time in Your Device settings, and the rest of the Application keeps working without them:

  • Photo library: to let You choose photos and videos for posts, chat messages and Your profile picture. Only the items You select are uploaded. The Application does not use Your camera.
  • Microphone: to record a voice message in a chat. Recording happens only after You start it, and the recording is uploaded only when You choose to send it.
  • Notifications: to alert You when a rest period in Your workout ends and when You receive a new chat message. These notifications are created on Your Device from data the Application has already received, so We do not collect or store a push notification token.
  • Bluetooth, Apple Health and Health Connect: to show Your heart rate during a workout, as described below.

The Application does not access Your Device's location. If You add a location to a post, You type it in Yourself, and the text You type is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim search service to suggest matching places.

The Application stores some data only on Your Device: Your login tokens (in the Device's secure storage), Your language, Your recent user searches and a random identifier that is used only to count unique readers of articles. You can clear Your search history in the Application. Your login tokens are removed when You log out, and all of this data is removed when You uninstall the Application.

Heart Rate Data (Bluetooth, Apple Health and Health Connect)

While You record a workout, the Application can show Your current heart rate. This feature is optional and works only after You choose a heart rate source and grant the related permission. You can use one of the following sources:

  • Bluetooth heart rate sensors: with Your permission to use Bluetooth, the Application searches for nearby sensors that use the standard Bluetooth heart rate service (for example, chest straps or armbands), connects to the closest one and reads only its heart rate measurements. We do not use Bluetooth to determine Your location. On Android 11 and older, the operating system requires the location permission for any Bluetooth search, but the Application does not access or use Your location.
  • Apple Health (iOS): with Your permission, the Application reads Your most recent heart rate samples from Apple Health. The Application only reads heart rate and never writes or changes any data in Apple Health.
  • Health Connect (Android): with Your permission, the Application reads Your most recent heart rate records from Health Connect. The Application only reads heart rate and never writes or changes any data in Health Connect.

Heart rate data is processed only on Your Device to display Your heart rate during a workout. It is not stored by the Application, it is not sent to Our servers or to any third party, and it is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling or any purpose other than showing it to You. We do not sell heart rate data or any other health information.

The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information obtained from Apple HealthKit is used in accordance with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines and is never used for advertising or disclosed to third parties.

You can stop the heart rate feature at any time by disconnecting the source in the Application, and You can revoke the permissions at any time in Your Device settings (Bluetooth), in the Health app under Sharing > Apps (Apple Health) or in the Health Connect app permissions (Android).
